# RACKP Patent Commitment

The RACK Protocol Project ("RACKP") has a pending patent application covering parts of the RACK Protocol, and may hold related patents in the future in any jurisdiction (together, the "RACKP Patents").

## Commitment

RACKP will not assert any RACKP Patent against any person or organization for:

1. implementing, deploying, or operating a Keeper (K), Actor (A), or Claimant (C) node;
2. anchoring evidence to a Keeper;
3. authoring or publishing Norm Profiles;
4. implementing or operating a Referee (R) node, including issuing billable artifacts (CONTRIBUTION_RESULTs and POH_CERTIFICATEs), in compliance with the [LICENSE](LICENSE), including its published fee schedule.

No acceptance, registration, or notice is required to rely on this commitment.

## Defensive termination

This commitment does not apply to any person or organization, or its affiliates, that brings a patent infringement claim against RACKP, or against anyone for an activity listed above, alleging that the RACK Protocol or an implementation of it infringes a patent. For that party, the commitment ends on the date the claim is filed.

## Successors

This commitment binds RACKP and any successor or assignee of the RACKP Patents. Any transfer of a RACKP Patent will be made subject to it.

## Scope

This commitment covers only the RACKP Patents and only the activities listed above. It does not grant rights under any other patent, and does not grant rights to the names "RACKP" or "RACK Protocol" or associated marks (see the restrictions in the [LICENSE](LICENSE)).
